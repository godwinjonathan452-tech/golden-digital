1. OUR VISION

GOLDEN DIGITAL is intended to become a global digital business ecosystem connecting businesses, buyers, sellers, entrepreneurs and creators through one unified platform.

Our goal is to make it possible for people around the world to promote businesses, create advertisements, sell products and services, communicate across languages and use intelligent digital tools.

The platform must be designed for international expansion.

2. CORE PLATFORM MODULES

GOLDEN CORE

The central coordination system connecting the platform's different services.

GOLDEN MARKET

GOLDEN TRANSLATE

Communication and translation across languages.

GOLDEN BUSINESS

Business management and seller tools.

3. ACCOUNT SYSTEM

Account registration must be required before users access GOLDEN services.

Proposed account information:

- Full name.
- Username.
- Email.
- Phone number.
- Password.
- Country.
- Preferred language.

Security requirements:

- Secure password hashing.
- Email and phone verification.
- Secure account recovery.
- Server-side account management.
- Protection against duplicate free-account abuse.
- Privacy-conscious device and account controls.

4. FREE AND PREMIUM AI ACCESS

Every new account receives five free AI uses in total.

The five uses are shared across GOLDEN AI features.

After the five free uses are exhausted, users must subscribe to Premium to continue using premium AI features.

Important decisions:

- Failed AI operations must not consume credits.
- Credits should only be deducted after successful operations.
- Credit accounting must happen securely on the server.
- Marketplace browsing and ordinary buyer-seller messaging should remain free.
- Premium pricing should display in the user's local currency.
- Subscription pricing and durations remain undecided.

The exact definition of one AI use must be established before implementation.

5. INTERNATIONAL PLATFORM

GOLDEN DIGITAL should support users from different countries.

Long-term requirements:

- Local currency display.
- International payment support.
- Multiple languages.
- Country-specific settings.
- International business accessibility.
- Support for cross-border commerce.

International expansion must account for payment availability, taxes, consumer protection, exchange rates and shipping regulations.

6. BUYER AND SELLER EXPERIENCE

A buyer should be able to:

1. Discover products.
2. View product information.
3. Contact the seller.
4. Ask questions.
5. Receive replies.
6. Complete purchases through supported payment methods.

A seller should be able to:

1. Create a business account.
2. Publish products or services.
3. Receive enquiries.
4. Communicate with buyers.
5. Manage listings.
6. Monitor business activity.

7. AI ADVERTISING EXPERIENCE

GOLDEN AI STUDIO should eventually allow users to create complete advertisements containing:

- Product images.
- Video scenes.
- Background music.
- Narration.
- AI-generated voice.
- Text and branding.

The goal is to produce advertisements with both visual and audio elements, rather than silent images alone.

8. SECURITY AND TRUST

GOLDEN SHIELD must protect buyers, sellers and the platform.

Requirements:

- Clear platform policies.
- Fraud prevention.
- Secure authentication.
- Suspicious activity detection.
- Protection against harassment.
- Reporting tools.
- Proportionate automated enforcement.
- Human review of ambiguous cases.
- Notice and appeals.
- Protection of personal information.

High-risk actions should be restricted when justified by reliable evidence. Weak signals alone must not automatically result in permanent account bans.

9. PAYMENTS AND PREMIUM MEMBERSHIP

GOLDEN PAY is intended to support secure payments.

Before real payment processing is launched, we must establish:

- Supported countries.
- Payment providers.
- Transaction fees.
- Refund procedures.
- Chargeback handling.
- Tax obligations.
- Subscription cancellation.
- Secure billing records.

No paid service or subscription should be introduced without first explaining its cost and obtaining approval.

Card details must not be stored directly by GOLDEN DIGITAL.
